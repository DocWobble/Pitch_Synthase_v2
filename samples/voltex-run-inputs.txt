FIELD: WHAT TO CONVEY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A toy so viscerally real and dangerous-feeling that kids cannot stop screaming
about it on camera, and parents rationalize the risk as memorable play. The
visual register must be hypercolor 90s radical toy ad: oversaturated neon
palette (hot pinks, electric blues, screaming yellows), diegetic action poses
where the kids are caught mid-chaos like Super Soaker box photography,
scorched-earth 'THIS IS THE MOST RADICAL THING' energy. The counterintuitive
challenge: market a genuinely high-tech plasma emitter entirely in the register
of a coke-fueled 90s children's toy campaign — no tech-lab aesthetics, no
adult credibility signaling, pure dangerous-fun toy aisle aggression. One
approach must emphasize that the product is so unbelievable you can barely
register photos of it as real — not stylized, not CGI, not illustrated: actual
photographic documentation of physics working, raw footage credibility, the
kind of image where you think the camera is lying but it isn't. The toy design
is translucent frosted bright-colored plastic (never black or silver), wide
flared muzzle, visible glowing cartridge bay, clearly and aggressively
non-threatening silhouette — closer to a handheld plasma globe than any
firearm.

VOLTEX — TECHNICAL ADDENDUM TO PITCH MATERIAL
Mechanism brief for generation purposes. Appended to elevator pitch as source
material.

WHAT IT ACTUALLY IS

Voltex is a modified Tesla coil coupled to an integrated magnetic accelerator,
purpose-built to ignite and launch self-stabilizing ball lightning. The
projectile is real plasma — not a laser, not an arc, not a beam — trapped in
a rotating magnetic vortex and accelerated down the barrel like a coilgun
round.

The key physics is rotating magnetic fields: not mechanical rotation, but a
phase envelope — an m=1 surface wave forced to propagate around a looped
surface (a spherical cap geometry), cycling fast enough to create a sealed
pocket in space. This is the same principle as whispering gallery mode
resonators — RF energy confined to a surface physically smaller than its own
wavelength, which forces evanescence rather than radiation. The result is a
toroidal maelstrom of short-range, high-intensity magnetic field that can hold
plasma suspended in midair without direct contact.

THE MECHANISM SEQUENCE

The barrel contains a birdcage resonator — an impeller coil that traces the
full length of the barrel in a tight helix, exactly like rifling. When the
circuit is activated, the resonator generates the rotating confinement field at
the center of the chamber. This is visually spectacular before the shot is even
fired: a visible plasma orb forms inside the translucent center globe, held
suspended in the middle of the housing.

The offhand operates a piezo striker mounted like a shotgun pump. Pressing it
ignites the air in the chamber into breakdown — the noble gas cartridge
determines the color. The rotating field holds the plasma ball in an excited,
coherent state while it charges.

Pulling the trigger changes the circuit state: the chamber confinement drops
and a discharge pulse propagates through the impeller coils in sequence,
creating a collimated rotating vortex down the barrel that accelerates the
plasma slug like a coilgun round. The plasma exits the muzzle still spinning,
still self-confined by its own magnetic curl. It begins decohering immediately
— like eddy currents dissipating, or a firework losing coherence — traveling
a variable distance before the vortex unravels and it dissipates.

HONEST LIMITATIONS (WHICH ARE ALSO THE SAFETY CASE)

The plasma slug has too little mass to carry meaningful thermal energy. It
cannot ignite a fuse, cannot start a fire, cannot sustain contact — it scorches
cardboard at close range and dissipates on contact. Accuracy is variable
because decoherence is environmentally sensitive. It requires both hands because
the offhand piezo is part of the firing sequence — you cannot operate it
single-handed. The two-hand form factor, combined with a body sized
specifically to prevent self-targeting, is not a compliance workaround; it is
a mechanical requirement of the design.

FORM FACTOR

Not a pistol. The closest existing cultural analog is the Heavy Plasma Cannon:
two-hand grip, wide rounded body, substantial length, large emitter. The body
is bright ASA plastic — not acrylic, which cannot withstand prolonged UV from
the plasma — in the color of the cartridge variant. All metal components are
sheathed in the housing; nothing electrically active is touchable during
operation. The translucent globe at the center of the housing is structural —
it is the visible charging chamber, and it glows the color of the loaded noble
gas as the plasma builds. The muzzle is wide and flared, the barrel visibly
long, and the overall silhouette reads as science equipment before it reads as
anything else. The device is specifically engineered to be too large for a
child to aim at themselves.

INVENTOR LINEAGE

The first Super Soaker was designed by a NASA engineer. The first Voltex was
designed by an engineer who would have been at NASA if NASA had still been
hiring in plasma confinement and field propulsion at the time they graduated.
The design philosophy is identical: take physics that belongs in a laboratory
and find the exact configuration that makes it safe, manufacturable, and
undeniably fun.
