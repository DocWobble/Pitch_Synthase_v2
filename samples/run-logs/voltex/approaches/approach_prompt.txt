WHAT IT ACTUALLY IS

THE MECHANISM SEQUENCE

The barrel contains a birdcage resonator — an impeller coil that traces the full length of the barrel in a tight helix, exactly like rifling. When the circuit is activated, the resonator generates the rotating confinement field at the center of the chamber. Pulling the trigger changes the circuit state: the chamber confinement drops and a discharge pulse propagates through the impeller coils in sequence, creating a collimated rotating vortex down the barrel that accelerates the plasma slug like a coilgun round. The plasma slug has too little mass to carry meaningful thermal energy.
